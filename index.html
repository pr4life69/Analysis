🧰 Other devices & platforms

Phones/tablets/laptops: they only show up here if they are advertising over Bluetooth LE; many phones randomize their ID and only advertise briefly.
iPhone/iPad: browsers can't scan Bluetooth. Use nRF Connect or LightBlue for Bluetooth, and Fing for devices on your Wi-Fi.
Wi-Fi network devices (cameras, smart plugs, etc.): use Fing or your router's connected-devices page.
Hidden cameras: most don't broadcast Bluetooth. Check the Wi-Fi device list for unknown devices, then do a physical check (flashlight for lens glints, look at chargers, smoke detectors, clocks, vents).
Chrome flag for Scan All: chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features

ℹ️ About Device Radar

This application detects wireless devices that your browser is permitted to access. It does not guarantee detection of hidden cameras, microphones, phones, or other electronics.

RSSI is a relative radio-signal measurement. Use it to tell whether a signal is getting stronger or weaker rather than as an exact distance measurement.
